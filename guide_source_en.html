1. DimFin Philosophy & Quick Start

Basics v1.4

👋 Welcome to DimFin!

DimFin was designed to free you from tedious bookkeeping. You don't need complex Excel sheets or confusing accounting software anymore.

1 What is your name?

On first launch, enter your name. The app will greet you with a friendly "Good morning, John! ☀️" every day.

2 Currency & Language (EUR, USD, UAH, PLN)

In Settings (⚙️) choose your preferred currency (Euro €, US Dollar $, Hryvnia ₴, Polish Złoty zł) and language: English 🇬🇧, Ukrainian 🇺🇦, or Polish 🇵🇱.

3 Set baseline monthly budgets

Set approximate limits for 3–4 key categories: Groceries, Utilities, Fuel, Shopping. This provides the baseline for smart analytics.

💡 Golden Rule: Don't try to configure everything on day one. Just log your daily spending — in 5–7 days DimFin will paint an accurate picture of your finances!

2. Receipts, Voice Input & Quick Expenses

Fast Logging

🧾 Receipts, QR Codes & OCR

Tap "Add from receipt" ➔ snap a photo, scan a fiscal QR code, or pick a receipt from your gallery. Text is recognized completely on-device without internet!

🔍 Up to 6x Zoom: view full-screen receipts, pinch-to-zoom or double-tap to inspect every line item.

🏪 Store, Timestamp & Geolocation

Tag popular store chains (Lidl, Biedronka, Carrefour, ALDI, Shell, ATB, Silpo...), preserve exact timestamps, and optionally save purchase GPS coordinates.

🎙️ Voice Input & 1-Tap Templates

Just say: "Coffee 4", "Taxi 15" or "Salary 2500", or tap pre-configured quick expense templates (Coffee, Transport) for instant 1-tap logging!

⚡ Pro Tip: You can attach or replace receipt photos at any time later directly from the transaction detail view!

3. Financial Radar & Smart Action Plan

On-Device Intelligence

🎯 How the Radar works in v1.4

The Radar continuously monitors your daily spending speed, remaining days in the month, and available balance. It shows an intuitive risk level (from "Calm 1/10" to "Risk 9/10").

⚡ Safe Daily Spending Limit

DimFin calculates how much you can comfortably spend each day to safely finish the month. Spent less today? Tomorrow's allowance automatically grows!

🧠 1-Tap "Apply Limit" Action

When an overspending risk is detected, the Radar suggests a clear solution:

  • Reduce spending pace: adjusts daily limit for upcoming days.
  • [Apply Limit] button: activates the target plan with an instant "[✓ Applied]" confirmation.
🛡️ Remember: The daily limit is a smart advisor, not a rigid warden. You are always in full control.

5. Biometric Lock, Flip-to-Hide & FAQ

Security & Privacy

🔒 Biometrics & "Flip-to-Hide" Mode

• Biometric Lock: Face Unlock or Fingerprint protects your financial records every time you open the app.
• Flip-to-Hide: placing your phone face down instantly masks all monetary figures into secure dots (••••)!

💾 Your Backups

Since DimFin does not store your records on any remote server, make backing up a healthy habit:

  • Go to Settings ➔ Export Backup.
  • Backup packages everything: transactions with receipt images, loans, savings goals, and budgets into a safe JSON file.
  • When moving to a new phone, simply tap "Import Backup".
❓ Can I link bank accounts?

No, and that is our greatest advantage. No third-party or scammer can ever access your banking credentials.

❓ Does DimFin support tablets & Foldables?

Yes! Version 1.4 features full large-screen optimization, multi-window Split-Screen, and free landscape rotation.
